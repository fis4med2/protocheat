# Legal notes

Anti-cheat vendors and publishers set the terms for online play. Bypassing them can lead to account bans and legal risk under local law, including anti-circumvention rules.

ProtoCheat does not circumvent technical protection. It reads local config, checks file presence, and prints guidance published by Valve, Epic and BattlEye. Kernel systems such as Vanguard and Ricochet are documented as unsupported under Proton. Valve states that kernel-space solutions are not supported and recommends user-space components.

Do not submit publisher binaries, keys, or dumped drivers.
